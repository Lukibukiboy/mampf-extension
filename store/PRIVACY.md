# MaMpf Enhancer: Privacy Policy

MaMpf Enhancer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

- It only runs on MaMpf video pages (`https://mampf.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/media/*`), where it changes the video's playback speed when you scroll over the speed selector.
- The only thing it saves is your popup settings (step sizes and fine-step key). They stay in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers. The developer never receives them.
- It makes no network requests and loads no remote code.

Questions: open an issue in this extension's GitHub repository.
